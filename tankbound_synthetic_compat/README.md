# Tankbound Synthetic Compatibility

This mod makes the Tankbound civic stay active after taking the Synthetic Ascension path.

## The Problem
In vanilla Stellaris, the Tankbound civic is automatically removed when you complete Synthetic Ascension, wasting all your investment in that civic.

## The Solution
This mod simply removes that restriction. The civic stays active after synthetic ascension with all its vanilla bonuses intact.

## Installation
1. Copy the `tankbound_synthetic_compat` folder to your Stellaris mod directory:
   - Windows: `%USERPROFILE%\Documents\Paradox Interactive\Stellaris\mod\`
2. Copy `tankbound_synthetic_compat.mod` to the same location
3. Enable the mod in the Stellaris launcher

## Compatibility
- Requires: Stellaris 3.x.x with Aquatics DLC
- Compatible with most other mods
- Simple override that just removes the civic swap restriction
