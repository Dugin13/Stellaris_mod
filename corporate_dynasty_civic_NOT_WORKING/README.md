# Corporate Dynasty Civic for Stellaris

This mod adds a new civic called **Corporate Dynasty** that enables hereditary succession for Corporate authorities.

## Features
- New civic: Corporate Dynasty
- Available only for Corporate authority
- Replaces elections with hereditary succession (heir system)
- Cannot be selected with Egalitarian ethics
- +1 Ruler skill level
- +5% Unity production

## How It Works
When you select the Corporate Dynasty civic:
- Your corporate ruler will have an heir instead of elections
- Power passes through hereditary succession
- Your ruler starts with +1 skill level
- The stability of dynastic rule provides a unity bonus

## Installation
1. Copy the `corporate_dynasty_civic` folder to your Stellaris mod directory:
   - Windows: `%USERPROFILE%\Documents\Paradox Interactive\Stellaris\mod\`
2. Copy `descriptor.mod` to the same location and rename it to `corporate_dynasty_civic.mod`
3. Enable the mod in the Stellaris launcher

## Usage
When creating a new empire:
1. Select "Corporate" as your authority
2. Choose "Corporate Dynasty" as one of your civics
3. You cannot select Egalitarian or Fanatic Egalitarian ethics with this civic
4. Your ruler will have an heir and hereditary succession

## Compatibility
- Designed for Stellaris 3.x.x
- Compatible with most other mods
- Cannot be used with Egalitarian ethics
- Works with vanilla Corporate authority

## Difference from Imperial Corporate Mod
- **This mod**: Adds a civic for vanilla Corporate authority
- **Imperial Corporate mod**: Creates a new authority type entirely

You can use both mods together for maximum flexibility!
